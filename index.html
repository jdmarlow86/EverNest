Good to know

Questions, answered simply.

Can I create a Nest and invite family?

You can try the Create a Nest demo, but real accounts, delivered email/text invitations, and cross-device family sharing are not connected yet. The demo stores information only in the current browser.

Is EverNest an app yet?

EverNest is starting as a website you can add to your device. The daily check-in saves your choice locally in this browser only; it does not send alerts or share the result with family members.

How do I add EverNest to my phone?

Tap “Add EverNest to this device.” If your browser supports installation, it will offer the install prompt. On iPhone or iPad, open the site in Safari, tap Share, then choose “Add to Home Screen.” On Android, open the browser menu and look for “Install app” or “Add to Home screen.” Menu wording varies by browser.

Will the shortcut work on a computer?

On supported desktop browsers, the button may offer an install option. Otherwise, bookmark the page or use your browser's option to create an app shortcut if available.

Does EverNest monitor health or contact emergency services?

No. This website does not monitor health, detect falls, send caregiver alerts, or contact emergency services. Never rely on it for urgent or medical needs.

Is my check-in shared with family?

No. This early version saves the check-in only in this browser on this device. Family sharing, accounts, and notifications are not connected yet. If you need help now, please contact someone you trust directly.
